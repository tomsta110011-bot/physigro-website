Your appointment

A straightforward place to start.

Bri will talk through what's going on, assess what needs looking at and work with you on a plan that makes sense for your goals and day-to-day life.

Initial appointment

Allow 60 minutes for your first physiotherapy appointment.

Follow-up appointment

Follow-ups run for 30–60 minutes depending on what is needed.

No private referral needed

You can book privately without a referral. A referral is only needed when claiming under an eligible Medicare GP plan.

Funding options

Private health, Medicare referrals, NDIS, Workers Comp and CTP clients are accepted.
