Women's pelvic health

Private, respectful pelvic health care.

Women's pelvic health appointments are designed to give you time to talk through what's going on, ask questions and receive care that is tailored to you.

Your appointment

Time to talk, ask questions and be heard.

Bri has a particular interest in women's pelvic health and understands that these appointments can cover topics that aren't always easy to bring up.

Initial assessment

Your first women's pelvic health appointment is 60 minutes.

Follow-up

Follow-up pelvic health appointments are also 60 minutes.

Across different life stages

Pelvic health care is available through pregnancy, after birth and at other stages of life.

No private referral needed

You can book privately without a referral. A referral is only needed when claiming under an eligible Medicare GP plan.
