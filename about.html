About Bri

Hey, I'm Bri.

I'm a born and bred Glen Innes girl, and while my career took me away from home for a little while, the plan was always to find my way back.

Brianna ShortSenior Physiotherapist · Bachelor of Physiotherapy (Hons)
The face behind Physigro

A local clinic, built for a local community.

“After spending several years working as a physiotherapist, I decided it was time to bring my knowledge, experience and passion back to the community that raised me. And just like that, Physigro Physiotherapy was born.”

My goal was pretty simple: to provide affordable, high-quality physiotherapy to our little corner of the world, because living rurally shouldn't mean missing out on great healthcare.

I have a particular soft spot for women's pelvic health and paediatrics, two areas that can be tricky to access in rural communities. I wanted to change that, while still offering physiotherapy for everyone, from the littlest movers to the young at heart.

“Physigro is all about keeping things friendly, approachable and a little bit fun. No scary medical jargon, no silly questions, just good-quality care with a familiar local face.”

Proudly local, passionate about people, and here to help our community move, play and grow.
