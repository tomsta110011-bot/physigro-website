Paediatric physiotherapy

Helping kids move, play and grow.

Paediatric physiotherapy can help babies, children and teens with movement, physical development, strength, balance, coordination and taking part in everyday activities.

Book paediatric physio →Call Bri
What paediatric physio can look at

Practical support for everyday movement.

Every child is different, so the assessment and plan will depend on their age, development, health history and what matters to the family.

Movement & development

Support where there are questions or concerns about gross motor skills, mobility or physical development.

Strength & balance

Working on physical skills that help children feel more confident moving and playing.

Coordination & participation

Goals can relate to school, sport, playground activities, hobbies or anything else that's important to the child and family.

Family-centred care

Plans should make sense outside the clinic too, with practical ideas families can use in everyday life.
