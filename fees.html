Funding & referrals

What Physigro accepts.

Private healthAll private health funds are accepted. Your rebate depends on your own level of cover.
MedicareMedicare referrals from GPs are accepted. A referral is required if you're claiming under a GP Chronic Condition Management Plan, often still called an EPC.
NDISNDIS clients are welcome. Get in touch if you'd like to confirm your funding arrangement before booking.
Workers Comp & CTPWorkers compensation and CTP clients are accepted. Contact Physigro if paperwork needs to be confirmed first.
Common questions

Before your appointment.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed for a private appointment. A referral is needed if you're using an eligible Medicare referral through your GP.

Can I claim through private health?

Physigro accepts all private health funds. The amount you can claim depends on your own policy and level of cover.

Can I book outside normal hours?

Appointments outside the usual Monday–Friday, 7:30am–6pm hours may be available with prior approval.

Cancellation policy

Life happens.

At Physigro Physiotherapy, we understand that life can be unpredictable. Emergencies happen, plans change, and sometimes appointments are simply forgotten — and that's okay.

We kindly ask that you provide at least 12 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. This allows us to offer your appointment time to another client who may be waiting.

We understand that providing notice isn't always possible, particularly in the case of illness, emergencies or unexpected circumstances.

Repeated late cancellations or missed appointments may incur a cancellation fee of 50% of the appointment cost. If applied, this fee must be paid in full before another appointment can be booked.

We will always consider individual circumstances with understanding and compassion. Our aim is not to penalise genuine emergencies or occasional forgetfulness, but to ensure appointment times are respected and available to those who need them.

Thank you for your understanding and for supporting our small business.
